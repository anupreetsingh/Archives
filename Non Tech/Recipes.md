## COOKING FUNDAMENTALS

### Frying Proteins

#### Coating

- **Cornstarch** is the extracted starch from corn and is used as a crispier replacement for flour in frying coatings. In many Asian contexts, the terms cornstarch and corn flour are used interchangeably. In the US, **corn flour** usually means finely ground corn, which is used in foods like cornbread and tortillas.

#### Leavening and Browning

**Baking soda** is pure sodium bicarbonate which when mixed with an acid like yogurt, vinegar, lemon juice, or buttermilk, releases CO2 and helps baked goods rise. **Baking powder** contains baking soda plus an acid, so it can react on its own when mixed with liquid and heated.

- **Baking soda** is very alkaline, so when it is used on its own, it raises the coating's pH. A higher pH speeds up the Maillard reaction, which gives the crust deeper browning and a darker, crunchier finish.
- **Baking powder** contains baking soda plus a balanced amount of acid, so it raises the coating's pH less. Its main effect is releasing CO2 bubbles, which makes the crust puffier, airier, and lighter in texture.
- **Using both together** gives lift from the baking powder and extra browning from a small amount of baking soda. Use baking soda sparingly because too much can make the coating taste alkaline.

### Sauces

#### 1. Marinara sauce-

- Saute Thinly chopped onions in olive oil
- Add chopped/crushed garlic when onions caramelize
- Add a lot of crushed tomatoes
- Add salt+oregano+chilli flakes
- Add sugar for reducing sourness
- Simmer and stir the mixer for about 2 hours until thick consistency is obtained

#### 2. Bolognese Sauce-

- Saute chopped onions, celery and carrots in olive oil
- Add chopped/crushed garlic when onions caramelise
- Add Meat(Beef, veal, pork) and saute
- Add a lot of crushed tomatoes
- Add salt+oregano+chilli flakes
- Add sugar for reducing sourness
- Simmer and stir the mixer for about 2 hours until thick consistency is obtained
- Add full cream at the end

- Melt butter

- Add little salt and black pepper for taste (also could add nutmeg if available)

#### 4. Alfredo Sauce-

- Melt butter
- Add Heavy cream
- Add Grated Parmesan until Smooth
- Add little salt and black pepper for taste (also could add nutmeg if available)

### Shoyu Ramen-

#### Broth

- Sauté garlic, ginger, and the light parts of the scallions until fragrant.
- Add mushrooms and sauté until slightly browned.
- Add chicken stock and simmer for 5-10 minutes.
- Add soy sauce to taste. Use dark soy sauce if you want a deeper color.
- Optional: Add shirodashi for extra dashi flavor, but reduce the soy sauce since shirodashi is salty.
- Taste, then season lightly with salt only if needed.
- Optional: Add a little mirin / sugar to balance the saltiness.

#### Noodles

- Boil the noodles until al dente. Fresh or dried ramen noodles from an Asian grocery store are preferred, but instant ramen noodles also work.

#### Garnishes

- Add one protein: chashu pork / chicken katsu / beef cutlet / tempura shrimp / tofu.
- Top with a soft-boiled egg, corn, and the green parts of the scallions.
- Optional: Blanch / sauté bok choy separately, then add when assembling.
- Optional: Add bean sprouts briefly at the end so they stay crunchy.
- Optional: Add nori.
- Finish with toasted sesame oil to help trap heat and add aroma.

### Miso Ramen

#### Broth

- Sauté the pork mince in a pan until browned.
- Add garlic, ginger, and the light parts of the scallions. Sauté until fragrant.
- Add mushrooms and sauté until slightly browned.
- Optional: Add chili bean paste or chili crisp for heat.
- Add chicken stock and simmer for 5-10 minutes.
- Lower the heat, then strain and stir in miso paste until dissolved. Dark miso is preferred if there is just one option, but a mix of light and dark miso also works.
- Taste, then season lightly with salt only if needed.

#### Noodles

- Boil the noodles until al dente. Fresh or dried ramen noodles from an Asian grocery store are preferred, but instant ramen noodles also work.

#### Garnishes

- Add one protein: chashu pork / beef cutlet / chicken cutlet / tempura shrimp / tofu.
- Top with a soft-boiled egg, corn, and the green parts of the scallions.
- Optional: Blanch / sauté bok choy separately, then add when assembling.
- Optional: Add bean sprouts briefly at the end so they stay crunchy.
- Optional: Add nori.
- Finish with toasted sesame oil to help trap heat and add aroma.
