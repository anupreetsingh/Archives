# Canadian PR

## Express Entry Steps

1. **Prepare your supporting documents.** Gather evidence for the information you will claim in your Express Entry profile:

   - **Work experience:** Employer reference letters and supporting pay stubs.
   - **English target — CLB 10:** IELTS General Training scores of at least 8.5 (Listening), 8.0 (Reading), 7.5 (Writing), and 7.5 (Speaking).
   - **French target — NCLC 9:** TCF Canada scores of 523–548 (Listening), 524–548 (Reading), 14–15 (Writing), and 14–15 (Speaking).
   - **Education outside Canada:** An Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) to establish the Canadian equivalency of your degree.
   - **Settlement funds:** For a family size of one, a balance of **CAD $16,000 budget** at the time of applying with bank statement for past six months. That money could come as an unconditional gift from your parents.

   The language scores above are your targets; minimum requirements depend on the program and draw category.

2. **Create and submit your Express Entry profile.** You must qualify for at least one of the three programs managed through Express Entry:

   - **Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSWP):** Your potential route based on overseas work experience; requires at least one continuous year of qualifying skilled work within the previous 10 years, the required language results, and at least **67/100 selection points**, alongside the other program requirements.
   - **Federal Skilled Trades Program (FSTP):** For qualifying skilled trades workers; requires at least two years of qualifying trade experience within the previous five years and a qualifying job offer or Canadian certificate of qualification, alongside the other program requirements.
   - **Canadian Experience Class (CEC):** Requires at least one year of qualifying Canadian skilled work experience within the previous three years, alongside the other program requirements; your overseas experience does not satisfy this requirement.

   If eligible, your profile enters the pool and IRCC assigns you a **CRS (Comprehensive Ranking System) score**, which you can view in your IRCC account. The FSWP selection score out of 100 determines program eligibility; the CRS score determines your ranking in the pool.

3. **Wait for Express Entry draws.** Each draw has a specified number of invitations. If there are **X invitations**, IRCC selects the **X highest-ranked candidates by CRS score who meet that draw’s eligibility requirements**. Ties at the cutoff are resolved using the profile submission date and time, with earlier submissions receiving priority.

   Your profile remains in the pool for **up to 12 months from submission**, provided you remain eligible. Missing a draw does not remove you from the pool; you continue to be considered for subsequent eligible draws. If your profile expires, you can submit a new one.

   Relevant draw types include:

   - **General draws:** Select the highest-ranked candidates across Express Entry programs; latest recorded CRS cutoff: **529 — April 23, 2024**.
   - **French-language draws:** Select eligible candidates with at least **NCLC 7 in all four French abilities**; latest recorded CRS cutoff: **382 — August 19, 2026**.
   - **STEM draws:** Select eligible candidates with at least **12 months of qualifying experience in a listed STEM occupation within the previous three years**; latest recorded CRS cutoff: **491 — April 11, 2024**. Currently eligible occupations include:

     - Architecture and science managers — **NOC 20011**
     - Cybersecurity specialists — **NOC 21220**
     - Civil engineers — **NOC 21300**
     - Mechanical engineers — **NOC 21301**
     - Electrical and electronics engineers — **NOC 21310**
     - Industrial and manufacturing engineers — **NOC 21321**
     - Geological engineers — **NOC 21331**
     - Civil engineering technologists and technicians — **NOC 22300**
     - Mechanical engineering technologists and technicians — **NOC 22301**
     - Electrical and electronics engineering technologists and technicians — **NOC 22310**
     - Insurance agents and brokers — **NOC 63100**

   Your single profile can qualify for multiple draw types. If selected, you receive an **ITA (Invitation to Apply)**.

   *Cutoffs and occupation list checked on September 14, 2026. The latest recorded general and STEM draws are from April 2024. Future cutoffs and category requirements may change.*

4. **Submit your permanent residence application after receiving an ITA.** Complete the application and provide the required supporting documents:

   - **Deadline:** Submit your complete application within **60 days** of receiving the ITA.
   - **Assessment:** IRCC verifies your documents, CRS claims, program/category eligibility, and admissibility.
   - **Processing:** Most Express Entry applications are processed within **six months of receiving a complete application**, but approval and that timeline are not guaranteed.
