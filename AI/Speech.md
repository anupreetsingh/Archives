Phoneme: The smallest sound possible in language

Grapheme: Written representation of that sound.

Intonation is the rise and fall of your voice's pitch.

Cadence is the overall rhythm and pacing of speech.

Enunciation is how clearly and distinctly you pronounce the words.

### Speech to Text

Transformer

Text
