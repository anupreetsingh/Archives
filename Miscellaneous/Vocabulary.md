
1. Etymology: Study of historical origin and evolution of words.

1. Idiom(Noun)/ਮੁਹਾਵਰਾ: A peculiar expression whose meaning is characterisitically tied to a particular language and cannot be fully understood by simply reading the words.

    Example: Kick the bucket(to die), Bite the bullet(do something unpleasant), Break the ice(start a convo), Break a leg(good luck).

1. Idiomatic(Adjective): Characteristic of the natural way native speakers use the language.

    Example: "I'd like to know what you think" instead of "I desire to know your thoughts on the matter"

1. Proverb/Aphorism/ਅਖਾਣ: A short memorable statement expressing general truth or wisdom. Example: "Knowledge is power", "Don't count your chickens before they hatch", "A stitch in time saves nine", etc

1. Adage/ਕਹਾਵਤ: An old well established proverb. Example: "Don't judge a book by its cover"

1. Maxim: A short memorable statement expressing a general rule of conduct. Example: Honesty is the best policy, Actions speak louder than words, A penny saved is a penny earned, etc

1. Motto: A maxim adopted by a person, family or organization to guide their conduct.

1. Argument(Noun): A claim supported by sound reasoning and evidence , designed to persuade an audience or prove a point.

1. Premise: A statement that you accept as true and use as a starting point to build an argument.
