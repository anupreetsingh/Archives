## Artillery

**Artillery** : Weapons designed to fire projectiles over a distance.

- **Cannon / gun**: usually fires at a lower angle, more directly toward the target.
- **Mortar**: fires at a high angle, sending the projectile in a steep arc.
- **Howitzer**: sits between a cannon and a mortar, firing shells in an arcing trajectory at medium to high angles.

All of these weapons fire projectiles along a **ballistic arc**, meaning the projectile curves through the air mainly under gravity after being launched.

A ballistic missile also follows a ballistic path, but it is not usually classified as normal artillery because it is rocket-powered and can travel much farther than conventional artillery.
